XITS is an OpenType implementation of STIX fonts with multi-script math support.

XITS Math contains symbols for mathematical and scientific notation with additional support for RTL scripts, including Arabic, Adlam and N'ko. The family includes five styles Regular, Bold, Italic, Unjoined Regular and Unjoined Italic. The unjoined varities are intended to support presentation of variables in N'ko where joining behavior would make individual variables appear as single element. In general the Regular style is used for mathematical symbols and functions. The Bold can optionally be used for functions which may be useful for N'ko to better distinguish them from variables. The Italic styles are used for variables.

To contribute, see github.com/JamraPatel/XITS-Math.
